# How badges display on your profile

The profile sidebar shows one icon per achievement, coloured by your highest tier, with a multiplier label for tiered badges below Gold tier display conventions. The Achievements tab shows the same set at full size.

Highlights (Pro, Developer Program Member, Public Sponsor, Security Advisory Credit, and others) display in their own Highlights section, separate from achievements. A complete profile in the collector sense means both sections filled: every earnable achievement at the highest tier you can reach, plus every highlight that applies to you.
