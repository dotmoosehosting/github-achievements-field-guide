# Writing answers that get accepted

The accepted answer is chosen by the person who asked, usually within a day. Patterns from answers that consistently win the mark:

- **Answer the question asked**, in the first two lines. Context and alternatives come after.
- **Show, do not describe.** A command, a code block, a screenshot description, or a link to the exact documentation section.
- **Name the version or plan** your answer applies to, so the asker can tell it matches their situation.
- **Say what success looks like.** The message, screen, or output that confirms the fix.
- **Be gracious.** The next person with this problem finds this thread through search, and tone is part of why maintainers keep Discussions enabled.
