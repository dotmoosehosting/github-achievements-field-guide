# Galaxy Brain Bronze: 8 accepted answers

Eight accepted answers is achievable in a few weeks of deliberate helping in repositories you know well:

- Turn on notifications for Discussions in two or three projects you use daily.
- Prefer questions you have personally hit and solved; your answer will carry the detail that gets it accepted.
- Include the version, the exact setting or command, and what the asker should see when it works.
- Answer early. The first correct, complete answer takes most acceptances.

Accepted answers are a reputation market: askers mark the answer that solved their problem, so clarity beats cleverness every time.
