# Open Sourcerer (unreleased)

**Status:** unreleased experiment, withdrawn shortly after it accidentally appeared in 2026. No public launch date.

**What it is expected to count:** pull requests merged in public repositories, the core open source contribution loop.

As with Heart On Your Sleeve, the preparation is the activity itself. Every merged public pull request is already on your record. Developers who have been contributing in public for years will likely qualify on day one if the badge launches, because GitHub's achievement system works from contribution history, not from activity after the announcement.
