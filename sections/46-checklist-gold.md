- [ ] Pull Shark: 1024 merged pull requests.

- [ ] Galaxy Brain: 32 accepted answers.

Gold is a career receipt. Nobody sensible optimises for Gold directly; it accumulates behind years of public work, which is exactly why it is worth displaying when it arrives.
