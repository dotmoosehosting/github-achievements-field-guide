# Reactions, and the heart in particular

GitHub reactions (thumbs up, heart, rocket, hooray, and others) are the lightweight social layer on issues, pull requests, comments, and discussions. They are also the expected basis of the unreleased Heart On Your Sleeve achievement.

Healthy reaction habits:

- Heart the releases, fixes, and answers that genuinely helped you. Maintainers notice, and it costs nothing.
- Use reactions instead of plus one comments; they keep threads readable.
- Your reaction history is part of your public activity record, which is why it may one day be counted retroactively.
