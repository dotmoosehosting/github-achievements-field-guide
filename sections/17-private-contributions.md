# The private contributions setting

Your profile has a setting to include private contributions in your contribution graph and some achievement calculations. It lives in the contribution settings on your profile page.

Key distinctions:

- The setting changes what counts and what displays; it never exposes private repository names or code.
- Starstruck is inherently public: a private repository cannot collect public stars.
- If you do most of your work in private repositories and your badges look frozen, this setting is the first thing to check.
