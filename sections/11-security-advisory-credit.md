# Highlight: Security Advisory Credit

**Type:** profile highlight, not an achievement badge. It appears under Highlights on a profile.

**How it is earned:** be credited on a published GitHub Security Advisory (GHSA) for a repository, typically as the reporter of the vulnerability.

The honest route is to find and responsibly report a real vulnerability, then be credited when the advisory publishes. Maintainers can also publish advisories for their own repositories documenting a vulnerability in their own code, crediting whoever reported it, which is how coordinated disclosure is supposed to work: the fix, the advisory, and the credit all land in public.

This repository carries a published advisory for its deliberately vulnerable training example, with the fix shipped alongside it, as a worked demonstration of the process end to end.
