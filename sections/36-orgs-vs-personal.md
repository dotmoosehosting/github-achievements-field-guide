# Achievements belong to personal accounts

Achievements and highlights attach to personal user accounts, not organizations. Work you do in an organization's repositories counts toward your personal achievements, because the pull requests, answers, and commits are authored by your account.

Organization profiles have their own different furniture (pinned repositories, people, packages) but no achievement tab. Splitting work across multiple personal accounts splits your counters too, which is one more reason GitHub's one personal account rule aligns with how the badge system is designed.
