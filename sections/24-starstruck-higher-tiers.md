# Starstruck Silver and Gold: 512 and 4096 stars

Silver at 512 stars puts a repository in the genuinely popular tier for its niche; Gold at 4096 is the territory of tools a whole ecosystem knows by name.

At these levels stars compound: popular repositories appear in search results, trending pages, awesome lists, and other people's blog posts, each of which brings the next cohort of stars. The maintainer's job shifts from promotion to stewardship: triage, releases, documentation, and keeping the project healthy enough that recommending it stays easy.

Very few accounts hold Starstruck Gold. Like Pull Shark Gold, it reads less as a badge someone chased and more as a receipt for a project that mattered to a lot of people.
