# Checking your achievements

Your full achievement list lives at:

```text
https://github.com/<your-username>?tab=achievements
```

The profile sidebar shows the same set in compact form, with the tier colour on each badge icon and the highest tier only. Hovering or tapping a badge shows its name.

If you believe you qualified and nothing shows:

1. Confirm the activity was on a public repository (or that private contributions are enabled where that applies).
2. Confirm the counts: thresholds, not vibes. Two merged pull requests, not one merged and one closed.
3. Wait a full day before concluding anything is broken.
4. Check GitHub's community discussions for an active badge display incident; there have been several.
