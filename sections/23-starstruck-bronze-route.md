# Starstruck Bronze: getting a repository to 128 stars

The jump from 16 to 128 stars is where Starstruck turns from a pleasant surprise into distribution work. Repositories that make it usually share a pattern:

- They solve one problem completely rather than five problems partially.
- The README shows the result before it explains the implementation.
- There is a release, a demo, or a live instance a visitor can touch within a minute.
- The author showed up where the audience is: the subreddit, forum, Discord, or publication for that niche, with a post written for that community.
- Follow-up issues and pull requests got fast, friendly responses, which converts visitors into stargazers and stargazers into contributors.

One honest launch post in the right community routinely outperforms months of passive waiting. What does not work: star exchanges, purchased stars, and alt accounts, all of which violate GitHub's terms and can be removed.
