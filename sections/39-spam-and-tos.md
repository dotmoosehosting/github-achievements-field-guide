# What not to do: spam, alts, and purchased engagement

GitHub's terms prohibit the obvious shortcuts, and they are also pointless:

- **Purchased stars** and star exchanges violate the acceptable use policies and are periodically purged.
- **Alt accounts** created to star your repositories, accept your answers, or co-author your commits breach the one personal account rule and produce badges that collapse under ten seconds of scrutiny.
- **Junk pull request floods** into other people's repositories are spam, full stop. Hacktoberfest tightened its rules precisely because of badge driven spam.

Every badge described in this guide can be advanced with real activity in your own projects and genuine contributions elsewhere. That is slower, and it is also the whole point of the system.
